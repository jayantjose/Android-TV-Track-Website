How to Limit YouTube Screen Time on Android TV & Google TV

Updated: November 2026 • 3 min read • Parental Guide

YouTube is the #1 screen time consumer for children on Smart TVs. Autoplay, endless algorithms, and bright content can turn a 20-minute cartoon into a 3-hour binge. Here is the easiest, remote-controlled way to enforce automatic daily limits and mandatory viewing breaks on Android TV and Google TV.

Why Built-In Smart TV Parental Controls Fall Short

Most parents look inside Android TV's system settings or Google TV's Kids Profiles hoping to find a timer for YouTube, only to find:

  • No Per-App Daily Limits: Google TV allows setting a total bedtime or a whole-device timer, but cannot restrict YouTube to 45 minutes while leaving educational apps or movies accessible.
  • Autoplay Bypasses: Children quickly learn to click recommended thumbnails, defeating manual agreements.
  • Parental Constant Monitoring: Parents are forced to check the clock, walk into the living room, and physically turn off the television—leading to arguments and tantrums.

Step-by-Step: How to Restrict YouTube in 3 Minutes

Follow these simple steps using your TV remote. No smartphone pairing, credit card, or account registration is needed.

Step 1

Install TV Timer on Your Smart TV

Open the Google Play Store on your Android TV, Xiaomi Mi TV, Sony Bravia, TCL, or Chromecast with Google TV. Search for "TV Timer: Parental Control" (or package com.tvtimer.parentalcontrol) and install it.

Step 2

Set Your Parental 4-Digit PIN

Launch the app. You will be prompted to create a 4-digit master PIN. The PIN interface is engineered specifically for your standard TV remote D-Pad (Up, Down, Left, Right, OK), so you never have to type on a sluggish on-screen keyboard.

Step 3

Enable the Accessibility Permission

TV Timer needs to know when YouTube is in the foreground. When prompted, click Enable Accessibility. Your TV will open Settings > Device Preferences > Accessibility. Find TV Timer Monitor Service and toggle it ON.

Note: In accordance with Google Play's strict security standards, TV Timer operates entirely on your TV. It does not record video, read screen pixels, or transmit any usage data over the internet.
Step 4

Set YouTube's Daily Limit & Session Breaks

In the TV Timer app dashboard:

  • Select YouTube (and/or YouTube Kids) from your installed apps list.
  • Set your daily limit (e.g., 45 minutes).
  • (Optional) Set a Continuous Session Break: For example, require a 15-minute rest after 30 minutes of non-stop viewing to prevent eye strain.
  • Click Save.

What Happens When the Daily YouTube Limit Expires?

As soon as the timer reaches 0:00:

  1. Instant Audio Mute & Interruption: The TV audio is immediately muted, and the active YouTube video stops.
  2. Home Screen Ejection: The app safely exits YouTube and returns to the TV home screen.
  3. Tamper-Resistant Remote PIN Lock: If a child attempts to click and relaunch YouTube, the TV Timer lock screen immediately interrupts with a PIN keypad. YouTube cannot be accessed until tomorrow's midnight reset—unless a parent enters the PIN.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can kids bypass the YouTube daily timer by restarting the TV?

No. TV Timer stores active watch time locally in persistent storage. If the TV is powered off or rebooted, the countdown resumes immediately from where it left off.

How can parents watch YouTube after the kids go to bed?

You can either enter your 4-digit PIN on the TV remote for a temporary bypass, or enable the built-in Bypass Schedule (e.g. 9:00 PM – 12:00 AM) where screen time tracking automatically pauses.

Which Smart TV brands are supported?

TV Timer works on any television or streaming stick running Android TV or Google TV (Android 7.0 / API 24 or higher), including Xiaomi Mi TV, Chromecast with Google TV, Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense, OnePlus TV, and Nvidia Shield.
