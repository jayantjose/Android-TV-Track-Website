Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about installing and using TV Timer on your Android TV.

Is TV Timer: Parental Control really 100% free?

Yes! There are no trials, no hidden subscriptions, and no paid tiers. All features—including daily limits, continuous cooldowns, and the bypass schedule—are completely free for everyone.

How do I enter the PIN using my TV remote?

The lock screen features a large, TV-optimized number grid. Simply navigate using your remote's D-Pad (Up, Down, Left, Right) and press the OK / Center button to enter each digit.

What apps can I set limits for?

You can set limits for any installed TV application, including YouTube, YouTube Kids, Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, JioCinema, Hotstar, and Android TV games.

How does the "Bypass Schedule" work?

If you don't want your own TV watching during work or school hours to count against your children's daily screen time limits, you can set a Bypass Schedule (e.g. Monday–Friday 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM). During these scheduled hours, timer tracking pauses automatically.

Why does the app require Accessibility permission?

Android TV requires an Accessibility Service to detect which TV application is currently active on your screen. This is used solely on your device to track time and enforce your limits. We never read, transmit, or record any screen content.
